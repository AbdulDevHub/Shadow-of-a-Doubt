## Overview

This repository contains the code for **Shadow of a Doubt**, a first-person magic shooter created in Unity. You play as Shadow, a clever black cat left alone in a haunted witch’s house for one long, perilous night. When ghosts begin invading the home, Shadow must harness elemental spells, fend off escalating waves of spirits, and confront a powerful twist awaiting the Witch’s return.

Here is the synopsis:  

```
Step into the paws of Shadow, an agile black cat armed with your witch’s enchanted wand. As the night begins, a sudden invasion of ghosts forces you to defend the house room by room. Each level introduces new elemental enemies and unlocks new spells to counter them. Survive waves of spirits, collect stolen potions, and prepare for the final showdown when the Witch returns—possessed by a malevolent force. Can you save her and prove your worth, or will the house fall to the shadows?
```

## Features

- **Elemental Spell Combat**  
  Cast fire, water, and wind spells to defeat different ghost types with unique weaknesses.

- **Wave-Based Progression**  
  Battle through three levels of increasing difficulty, unlocking new spells after each stage.

- **Playable Cat Protagonist**  
  Experience FPS gameplay from the low-to-the-ground perspective of a mischievous feline.

- **Potions & Resource Management**  
  Collect dropped health and mana potions, including Ultimate Potions that grant brief invincibility.

- **Climactic Final Boss**  
  Face off against a possessed Witch with changing elemental shields, summoned spirits, and AoE attacks.

- **Multiple Endings**  
  Your performance—time, deaths, and score—shapes the final outcome.
